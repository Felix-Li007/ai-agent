Southeast Asia Population Summary
===================================

Overview:
Southeast Asia is the geographical southeastern region of Asia, consisting of countries situated south of China, east of the Indian subcontinent, and northwest of Australia.

Total Population:
- The estimated total population of Southeast Asia is approximately 685–705 million people (as of 2024-2025).
- The ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations) member states have a combined population of about 694 million as of 2024, representing roughly 8.5% of the world's population.
- Some estimates place the Southeast Asia population at approximately 704.8 million.

Countries (by approximate population size, largest to smallest):
1. Indonesia – ~283.5 million
2. Philippines – ~117 million
3. Vietnam – ~100 million
4. Thailand – ~71 million
5. Myanmar – ~55 million
6. Malaysia – ~34 million
7. Cambodia – ~17 million
8. Laos – ~7.5 million
9. Singapore – ~5.9 million
10. Timor-Leste (East Timor) – ~1.4 million
11. Brunei – ~0.45 million

Key Facts:
- Southeast Asia is one of the most populous and demographically significant regions in the world.
- The region accounts for approximately 8.4–8.5% of the global population.
- Indonesia is by far the most populous country in the region, making up over 40% of the total population.
- The region's population is projected to continue growing, with estimates of over 767 million projected by 2046.

Sources:
- United Nations World Population Prospects
- ASEAN Statistical Yearbook 2024
- International Energy Agency data on Southeast Asia
- CIA World Factbook
- World Bank data
